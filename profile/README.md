# TemsAI

**AI-native Connected Worker platform for frontline manufacturing operations.**

TemsAI captures operational knowledge from scattered sources, turns it into digital work
instructions, and guides, trains and supports every worker on the shop floor — from
onboarding to expert performance.

### What we build

- **Digital Work Instructions** — create SOPs, smart forms and checklists in minutes from
  Excel, Word, PDF, images and video.
- **Workflow Builder** — digitize routine workflows and go paperless, with real-time data
  capture and traceability.
- **GenAI Assistant** — industrial-grade generative AI that answers workers' questions at
  the point of work.
- **AI Agents** — custom agents that turn operational knowledge into action across
  training, quality, safety and maintenance.

### Where it's used

Quality · Maintenance (autonomous and preventive) · Safety · Skill management and
workforce development · OEM technical documentation
